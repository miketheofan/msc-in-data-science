# Risk Analysis

**Institution**: Athens University of Economics and Business (AUEB)
**Course**: Introduction to Quantitative Finance and Risk Management

## Project: Portfolio Choice, Diversification and CAPM Evaluation

Construction and evaluation of mean-variance optimal portfolios using 10 stocks across distinct GICS sectors. Covers diversification analysis, efficient frontier computation (with and without short-selling), CAPM beta estimation, and out-of-sample portfolio performance evaluation over Q1 2026.

**Technologies**: Python, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, yfinance, SciPy, statsmodels
